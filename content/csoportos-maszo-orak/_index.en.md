### Prices:

Group session fee: **8,000 HUF / session**.

Payment in the first month can be made per session by bank transfer or in cash. Thereafter, the monthly climbing session fee is **32,000 HUF, covering 4 sessions**.

The 4 sessions can be used within 5 weeks.

Valid from 1 June 2026.


### Required equipment

* **Indoor shoes**, preferably with light-coloured soles. Climbing in socks is also possible, though less comfortable.
* **Comfortable play clothes**
* Optionally, a spill-proof water bottle

### Parents' presence

During the first month, we warmly welcome parents to attend the sessions, so that the child can start the shared work in a safe, calm atmosphere. As trust develops, in the interest of session effectiveness, we ask parents to wait for their child in the comfortable waiting area while the child participates independently in the sessions.
