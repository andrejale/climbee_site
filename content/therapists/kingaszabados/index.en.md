# About Me

Working with children has always been a dream of mine, so it is a great joy to apply this method. I consider myself fortunate to have learned the Tunyogi Therapeutic Play Therapy from Erzsébet Tunyogi. With this method, we can achieve great progress in children in a playful, motivating way. My goal is for the parent and child to learn to play together, thereby reaching the desired development. I believe it is important to see the child and parent as part of the family unit, and not to focus only on the child's current difficulties.
