I joyfully accompany those who are committed to the path of personal growth and self-awareness and wish to bring positive changes into their lives. In my work I create a safe, accepting atmosphere where you can freely share your inner world — and difficulties can be transformed together into new opportunities. I completed my psychology and behaviour analysis degrees at the University of Pécs, then my skills development trainer qualification at the Budapest University of Technology and Economics, and finally deepened my knowledge of emotional intelligence and personality development methods at the Personality Development Academy. I work from an integrative perspective, using tools such as family and systemic constellation, symbol therapy, cognitive techniques, body work, empty chair technique, dream and drawing analysis, and inner child work. You are welcome to reach out regarding stress, lack of self-confidence, difficulty making decisions, burnout, relationship and workplace problems, loss, feeling stuck, or a desire for communication growth. I warmly look forward to a supportive, constructive conversation with you!

# More About Me

I first completed a degree in behaviour analysis and then in psychology at the Faculty of Humanities, University of Pécs. Following that, in keeping with my interests, I turned towards groups and trained as a "Skills Development Trainer" at the Department of Ergonomics and Psychology at the Budapest University of Technology and Economics. My thirst for knowledge did not stop there, so as the next step in my development I chose the "Emotional Intelligence Developer – Personality Developer" professional qualification at the Personality Development Academy. There I was able to learn wonderful, integrative techniques such as Hellinger family, systemic and organisational constellation, symbol therapy and cognitive methods, body work, empty chair technique, dream analysis, drawing analysis, and inner child work.

If you are interested in personality development and self-knowledge that promises positive change in your life — you've come to the right place! I will gladly support you in achieving your goals.
My broad knowledge base of theoretical learning and practical techniques enables me to provide help in the following areas:

- you are often stressed, tense, or irritable
- you need life coaching
- you struggle with relationship difficulties (romantic relationships, parent-child relationships, connection difficulties)
- you constantly find yourself in workplace conflicts
- you lack self-confidence at work or in your personal life
- you feel you always have to meet expectations but cannot
- you find it hard to make decisions, and uncertainty often troubles you
- you have reached an important milestone in life but don't know what to do next
- you feel lonely and isolated
- learned family patterns are making your life difficult
- you would like to develop your communication and grow in healthy self-assertion
- you want to strengthen your self-reflection
- you find it hard to experience your own feelings and also have difficulties recognising and understanding others' emotions
- you have experienced a loss and are grieving
- you feel you have stepped onto the path of burnout or depression and wish to prevent it

My goal is to create a trusting atmosphere in which you can comfortably share your experiences with me, and together we can turn problems into opportunities for growth.

I warmly look forward to a supportive conversation with you!
