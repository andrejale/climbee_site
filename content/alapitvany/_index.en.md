The Foundation's mission is to make climbing-wall-based, movement-centred development as widely accessible as possible for children, in an affordable and professionally grounded form.

We place particular emphasis on supporting children for whom climbing-wall movement development can be especially important for their growth, yet for whom a traditional sports or climbing gym environment is not always suitable. This is especially true for neurodivergent children, for whom increased sensory load, noise, and less structured environments can be inhibiting factors.

The Foundation's aspiration is that these children too can access the experience of climbing-wall movement development, and grow within a framework adapted to their individual needs.
