Sessions for kindergarten groups take place on a pre-arranged, weekly basis. The programme fits into the institution's daily schedule in a similar way to other extracurricular activities (e.g. swimming).
ClimBee's kindergarten programmes are delivered in a structured, playful framework where movement, sensory experience, and the development of cognitive skills appear in an integrated way.

### About the sessions

The programme's professional foundation is the integrated approach of special education, drama-based pedagogy, and experiential pedagogy. Each session lasts 50 minutes, during which children participate in small groups through tasks adapted to their age and developmental level. The development focus is on supporting complex neurological maturation, with particular emphasis on:

* developing movement coordination and balance
* strengthening attentional functions and self-regulation
* supporting sensory integration
* developing communication and cooperation skills
* refining body awareness and spatial orientation

We warmly welcome kindergartens to apply and get in touch about collaboration. To ensure smooth implementation, we can also arrange transport for children in a minibus designed for this purpose. If institutions wish, we are happy to hold a demonstration day, which can take place at our centre or at the kindergarten. If needed, we can also come to you, as we have a mobile climbing wall system designed to be mounted on wall bars, which can be used safely for on-site demonstration sessions.
